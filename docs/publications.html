Code

VBC — Vine copula bias correction for climate data; corrects the multivariate distribution of heavy-tailed, zero-inflated and continuous climate variables. Accompanies the JRSS-C paper above.

FACT — Feature Attributions for Clustering; transfers algorithm-agnostic interpretability methods to clustering tasks. Accompanies the xAI 2023 paper above. (Formerly on CRAN; source now maintained on GitHub.)

FuzzyDBScan — Fuzzy clustering with DBScan. (Formerly on CRAN; source now maintained on GitHub.)
